🌿 BONOLATA: Autonomous Timber Chain of Custody

An enterprise-grade, decentralized verification platform designed to combat illegal deforestation, monitor physical timber assets via IoT telemetry, ensure driver vigilance through Edge AI computer vision, and guarantee chain-of-custody integrity with immutable cryptographic hashing.

Architecture & Core Systems

🌿 BONOLATA establishes an end-to-end audit trail for timber transportation using four foundational pillars:

🛰️ IoT Route Geofencing Monitors GPS telemetry and flags route deviations or unauthorized perimeter exits.
👁️ Edge AI Driver Monitor Calculates real-time Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) via MediaPipe to prevent fatigue-induced accidents.
⛓️ SHA-256 Ledger Every custody transfer, checkpoint clearance, and driver alert is immutably cryptographically sealed.
🤖 Charulata AI Assistant RAG-powered conversational interface for instant compliance querying and dispatch diagnostics.
